In Scorer World, open Help > Settings or Leaderboard and choose Stop sharing & remove. Keep the app until removal completes. You can remove your public records even when public features are locked. If you lost the app or cannot remove a record, contact the address above with the public name, team, category (Season or Career), season and division. Do not send passwords, adult PINs or administrator/ownership tokens. We assess requests without disclosing another person’s data. Scorer World has no email/password account. Uninstalling does not erase public records. Requests concerning retained records from earlier Scorer test builds can use the same contact.

What is removed

Stop sharing & remove deletes your Season and Career records, including the associated nickname, team and results, and reports associated with your publisher. Offline requests are retried while the app runs; keep the app until removal completes.

Usage statistics and retention

In Help > Settings, Stop Statistics & Erase Unsent stops future collection and deletes pending counters without requiring a PIN. A request already transmitted cannot be recalled. Unsent local counters expire after seven days. Server counters are combined by UTC receipt day and retained for 90 days; these combined totals cannot be traced back to remove an individual contribution. Managed server recovery backups contain aggregate counters only, exclude public records, reports, privacy requests and tokens, and expire after seven days.

Other retained information

Moderation reports are retained for up to 30 days with periodic cleanup, unless removed with the associated publisher. A suspended public publisher ID remains until the suspension is lifted to prevent re-publication. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations. Cloudflare processes connection and security information under its own terms; its retention is separate from our aggregate-counter retention. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Support requests

We assess requests and normally respond within one month, explaining any lawful extension. We ask only for proportionate information needed to establish authority and locate the relevant data. Do not send passwords, PINs, full career files or administrator/ownership tokens.
