Scorer World Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy · Community Rules · Data and Record Removal

Responsible developer: Gonçalo Pereira

Privacy and support: goncalo@clemir.com

Effective: 2026-11-11

Scorer World (com.clemir.scorerworld) is a free football career game with an included local names editor. It has no adverts, advertising SDKs or in-app purchases. This policy covers Scorer World and the retained records of earlier Scorer test builds. The separate Free edition is not a Google Play launch product.

App version compatibility

At startup, on returning to the app and occasionally while menus are open, Scorer requests a public minimum-version policy from stats.clemir.com. The installed version is compared locally; the request contains no player name, career, usage counters, advertising ID or unique device identifier. Cloudflare processes connection information as described below. This compatibility check is separate from optional usage statistics. A valid policy is cached locally: offline play remains available unless an already known minimum version requires an update. The update button opens the official Google Play listing; the app does not download or install APK files itself.

Optional usage statistics

Usage statistics are off by default, including after upgrading from earlier mandatory-statistics test builds. The launch acknowledgment does not enable them. An adult may separately allow statistics in Help > Settings after the disclosure and adult PIN. Playing, training and saving work without this choice. Stop Statistics & Erase Unsent stops future collection and deletes pending counters; it needs no PIN. A request already transmitted cannot be recalled. The nine optional numeric counters are app openings and normal closes, new and loaded careers, completed training and matches, goals and save/load failures. Recipient: stats.clemir.com; purpose: improve and maintain the game. No player name, career file, free text, exact event time or device/session identifier is in the counter payload. Cloudflare processes connection data including IP addresses. Local unsent counts expire after seven days; own server totals are combined by UTC receipt day and retained for 90 days. Combined totals cannot be traced back to remove an individual contribution. Delays or uncertain acknowledgments may duplicate events. Earlier test builds used automatic counters; update to the current app.

Optional public records

Adult-managed access, community rules and explicit Share precede publication of the chosen player nickname, team, season, matches, goals and edition/country/division. The Season category contains completed-season results; the separately enabled Career category contains cumulative club-career matches and goals, not international totals. Use a nickname, never personal contact or school details. New or changed submissions await moderator review; scores are self-reported. A private random ownership token authorizes uploads/removal; the server stores its hash. Opaque public publisher/record IDs support reports and blocking, but cannot authorize deletion. Records remain until withdrawal or moderation.

Children and local adult controls

Scorer World includes children and teenagers in its audience. Public record sharing is locked by default. The public table can be viewed without enabling sharing. An adult must complete a local adult-age declaration and configure/unlock a PIN before sharing; the date of birth is checked locally and discarded, never transmitted. This is a local access safeguard, not verified parental identity or proof of parental consent. An adult should supervise the decision and use pseudonyms, never a child’s full name, school or contact details. Sharing requires community rules and an explicit action; submissions await moderation. Reports, blocking, withdrawal and support are available. Usage statistics are optional, off by default and require a separate adult-managed opt-in. The game can be played without them. Shared names must be pseudonyms, not personal information. A safety reminder precedes sharing. We do not collect location, contacts, advertising IDs or behavioural profiles.

Adult PINs are stored as salted hashes on the device. Blocked publisher IDs and safety preferences also remain local. An adult can lock public access and queue withdrawal of records. PINs are never published, exported or sent to the server.

Reports, moderation and removal

Report/block tools are inside the app. Reports transmit the reporter token, target public publisher ID and a fixed reason; the server keeps the hashed reporter identity, target and reason for up to 30 days with periodic cleanup. Moderators may reject/erase records and suspend publishers. A suspended public ID remains until lifted to prevent re-publication. Stop sharing & remove deletes both Season and Career records and reports associated with the publisher, and is available even when public features are locked. Offline removal is retried while the app runs; keep the app until removal completes. If app data or the ownership credential is lost, contact support without sending secret tokens. Uninstalling does not remove public records.

Included names editor

The editor changes club, league and player display names locally. JSON document imports are previewed before applying. Editing does not change match statistics. There is no automatic upload of name packs or career files. Edited team/player names can appear in a public Season or Career record only through the separately enabled record-sharing feature.

Local careers, exports and backups

Careers remain on-device; there is no automatic cloud career upload or email/password account. English is the default; language selection and optional phone-language detection are local. Android app backup is disabled. User-selected career exports contain player names and game progress; keep them private and choose a trusted document provider, which may itself use cloud storage. Exports exclude adult controls, usage counters and online ownership credentials. Only the same edition can import a career. Uninstall removes private local files, not exported documents. Managed server recovery backups contain aggregate counters only, exclude public records/reports/privacy requests/tokens, and expire after seven days.

Local purchases and feedback

Career wages, purchases and estate portraits are fictional and stored locally. There are no real payments, user-photo uploads or automatic financial-data transmissions. Feedback opens a draft in your external email app with a subject; you choose whether to send it. No save, logs or attachments are added automatically. If you send email, the developer processes your reply address and message to answer you.

Transport and Cloudflare

HTTPS validates certificates. The server is operated by the developer and uses Cloudflare Tunnel/CDN. Our collector suppresses IP, user-agent and raw-request logging. This does not mean the network provider processes no personal data.

The developer has confirmed that Cloudflare is used only for Tunnel/proxy routing and protection for stats.clemir.com; Cloudflare Web Analytics, Logpush and other optional collection services are not enabled. Cloudflare necessarily processes IP addresses and connection/security information and may retain service data under its own published terms and privacy policy. Its role varies by data category, including processor for customer content and controller for some service data. Our collector does not retain raw request, IP or user-agent logs. We do not claim Cloudflare retains no logs or that its service-data retention equals our 90-day aggregate retention. No advertising, analytics SDK or third-party marketing tracker is embedded in the app. Public privacy, community and removal pages are also hosted on GitHub Pages at pereiragoncalo.github.io/Scorer. When you visit those pages, GitHub processes web request information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. These static pages contain no advertising, embedded analytics, contact form or game-record uploads. This website hosting is separate from gameplay requests to stats.clemir.com.

International processing

The developer operates the collector; Cloudflare routes traffic through an international network and may process connection data outside your country, including outside the EEA. EU-only residency is not promised. Cloudflare publishes a Data Processing Addendum and information about European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable to the relevant entities and transfers, Data Privacy Framework participation. These mechanisms apply according to the provider’s actual terms and applicable law; this policy does not claim an individual account audit or a separately verified contract. See the official provider references below and contact the developer about applicable safeguards.

Legal basis and rights

Optional usage statistics are based on a separate voluntary adult-managed opt-in where applicable. They are disabled by default; acknowledging the launch notice is not consent to statistics. The choice can be withdrawn in Help > Settings without affecting play. Public record sharing requires a separate adult-managed choice, community rules and explicit Share; sharing can be stopped and records removed. Necessary service security and moderation are performed to operate that requested feature and protect users, subject to applicable law. Support correspondence is processed to answer the request and comply with legal obligations. Applicable child-protection, parental-authorization and data-protection rights take precedence; a local PIN does not verify parental identity. Contact goncalo@clemir.com for assistance.

Contact goncalo@clemir.com for information, access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability where applicable, or assistance with a child’s privacy. We assess requests and normally respond within one month, with any lawful extension explained. We request only proportionate information needed to establish authority and locate the relevant data. Combined anonymous totals cannot be separated into individual contributions. Email correspondence is used to answer the request and retained only as needed for handling and legal obligations. Do not send passwords, PINs, full career files or administrator/ownership tokens. You may complain to your competent data-protection authority, including the Portuguese CNPD (www.cnpd.pt). There is no sale of personal data, behavioural advertising or automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this policy and, where required, an updated in-app notice before new processing. This policy is effective from the date above.
