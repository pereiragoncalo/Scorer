# Scorer World 1.0 (1030020)

Adds shallow rectangular (1.80 m) and deep triangular (3.50 m) stadium nets,
with both square and hexagonal mesh. Existing shapes remain available. All nets
use three independent rear support poles and cords; triangular stanchions are removed.
Net profiles, visible mesh, post-goal ball contact and cloth share each stadium depth.

Includes ball-driven net deformation, an end-of-season review with the coach,
results, scorer position and next-season objectives, and per-season optional
leaderboard publication. Two global Division 1/2 boards show only nickname and
league goals. Publishing asks for a name after a choice, without DOB/PIN fields;
legacy records are not automatically copied. The accompanying additive backend
update is required for v5 boards; earlier apps retain v3/v4 access.

Spreads goal events more naturally without changing the scoring model. Calendar
v4 reduces repeated home/away pairs across all clubs, with mirrored same-order
legs. Only compatible in-progress saves migrate; other saves change next season.
Same app package and owner signing identity; update without uninstalling.

Internal test candidate. Compilation is not Google Play availability. Keep the
mandatory minimum at zero until the owner confirms this exact version is offered
on all intended handsets/tracks. No advertising or billing SDKs are included.
