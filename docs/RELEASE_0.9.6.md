# Scorer World 0.9.6 — playoff and national-team iteration

This QA iteration adds played promotion and relegation brackets to the existing
two-division career engine. It does **not** complete the owner's expanded request
for real domestic formats in all212 countries. Group stages, splits, period
qualifiers, real country capacities and many country rules still need integration.
No country is certified as having its full real domestic format by this release.

## Implemented and tested

- French ladder:4th hosts5th, winner visits3rd, winner plays the upper division's
  third-bottom club over two legs. Upper clubs remain up if they win.
- Portugal/Germany cross-division ties, including the reserve boundary. Portuguese
  venue order is seeded/drawn; Germany's upper club hosts the first leg. Portuguese
  B-team identities cannot take first-division promotion places.
- Championship2026/27 six-team field:3rd–8th, two preliminary games, two-leg
  semifinals, neutral single final. Source: EFL's5March2026 announcement; its
  warning that exact final details were to be confirmed is retained in research.
- Spain's four-team promotion bracket; Italy's points-threshold eligibility,
  preliminary rounds/byes, rank-based aggregate decisions and conditional playout;
  Brazil's two independent3rd–6th/4th–5th ties for two further places.
- The configured access models also cover Scotland, Norway, Sweden, Finland,
  Ireland, Northern Ireland, Switzerland, Czechia, Turkey, Belgium, Japan and
  Estonia. These remain access adaptations on the existing league tables; they
  are not full national format implementations. Argentina's draft reduced bracket
  still derives its seeds from a combined table, not the real two-zone season.
- All bracket fixtures, scores, byes, aggregate decisions and movement survive
  save/load. Both boundaries are frozen before moving clubs; no same-rollover
  double drop. Reserve survivors keep their contract; losing clubs below2 become
  free agents. Closed access models no longer invent automatic promotion in
  United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand or Mexico.
- A positive goal total at competition rank1–3 in the player's current first
  division permits a call-up for the player's nationality, including when playing
  abroad. Ties at3rd qualify. No morale, recent-form or minimum-match gate.
- Window selections persist for the whole window; international appearances and
  goals update national counters only. Future friendlies and national games are
  visible before eligibility. Tuesday/Friday national fixtures are ordered against
  the existing club slots, so a Friday game cannot preempt Thursday's league game.
- Men's FIFA windows2025–2030 are pinned from the April2026 official PDF. Asian
  Cup2027 uses January–February; Gold Cup/AFCON2027 use their published summer
  ranges. Match draws/dates inside ranges are generated by the game, not a replica
  of every announced international fixture. Dates beyond published coverage and
  unannounced finals are projections. Existing drawn old-save competitions remain
  compatible; the game competition models are not all official2026 formats.
