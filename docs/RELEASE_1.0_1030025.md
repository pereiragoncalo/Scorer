# Scorer World 1.0 (1030025)

Choosing an initial/transfer offer now opens a club meeting. Hearing a club does
not sign, switch clubs, award assets or consume career RNG. Each club retains
its own proposal, request and response, with up to three deterministic rounds.
The player requests1–5 actual calendar years, higher monthly wages, housing,
a car or squad investment. The agent advises on affordability, commitment and
age-based term limits. The club accepts or returns a specific counter-offer.
Only the displayed club offer is signed after an explicit signature action.

Accepted housing/car benefits become owned signing benefits; funded squad
investment increases club strength. Wages remain the signed monthly amount,
not a fresh performance-based quote on each save. Full year anniversaries cover
midseason contracts and leap days. The profile shows compact contract years;
offers/agent/meeting show duration and the actual end date in nine languages.

Expiry stops wages after the contract period and requires renewal/new agreement
before match/training. Existing club/history is retained until the choice;
out-of-contract players may hear other offers outside normal transfer windows.
Same-club renewal preserves coach, cup registration and transfer count. Existing
careers keep wages/assets and gain two future years when no old duration exists.
Nested terms/meetings are bounded/validated and survive save/load and career
export/import for an existing career. Initial negotiations remain unsigned
setup drafts until the first signature, matching the existing first-save flow. Failed signing rolls back wages/assets/club strength/development/
coach/RNG. New-career statistics count only the explicit first signature.
