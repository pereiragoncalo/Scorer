# Scorer World0.9.40 QA — optional statistics and policy update

Statistics are now optional and off by default, including upgrades from old
mandatory-statistics builds. An adult can explicitly enable them separately in
Help → Settings after reading the disclosure and entering the adult PIN. Stop
Statistics & Erase Unsent works without a PIN and does not affect the career.
Acknowledging the compact startup notice does not enable analytics or sharing.

Public Season/Career records retain separate adult-managed sharing, terms,
pre-moderation, reports, blocking and removal. The online-safety reminder is now
explicit about public visibility and real-world personal-information risks.
The new notices are available in all9UI languages.

English public policy covers optional statistics, Season/Career categories,
English default/local language settings, fictional local purchases and email
feedback. A combined owner-run bastion installer updates public pages and the
v3/v4 board with transactional file rollback on failed local checks. Public
HTTPS access and actual ongoing moderation must still be verified by the owner.

Clubs are fictional, as confirmed by the owner. Local editor/import remain.
No changes to finishing physics, competition simulation, saves or purchase rules.
This is a DEBUG-signed QA build, not the signed Play1.0 AAB or Google approval.
Physical Android/Play pre-launch acceptance and Console forms remain pending.
