SCORER is a football striker career game. Free play has one playable division.
An optional one-time World purchase unlocks fictional worldwide leagues, the local
name editor and ad-free play in the same application. We do not create SCORER accounts.

Player names, career progress, league tables and edited names are stored privately
on your device. We do not upload these to our servers or advertising services. The
phone's language/region is read locally to suggest a starting country; the game UI
remains English. No GPS location permission is requested. You can choose local JSON
files for World names using Android's document picker; those files are only processed
after validation and confirmation. A chosen cloud document provider has its own policy.

Free play may show Google AdMob advertisements: a banner on the entry menu, full-screen
advertisements between completed matches and optional rewarded advertising in training.
No ad interrupts a shot. Ads depend on availability/consent and do not block offline
Free play. Requests are configured for children/under-age users, general-audience
content and non-personalised ads. We do not request Android Advertising ID or Privacy
Sandbox advertising permissions. Google may nonetheless process IP addresses, approximate
location derived from IP, app/device information, diagnostics and ad interactions.
Review Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Available consent/privacy
options can be reached inside the game. [CONFIRM FINAL GOOGLE/PROCESSOR DISCLOSURES]

Google Play handles the optional World payment, account and payment confirmation.
SCORER never receives payment card details. To verify the non-consumable purchase,
our [DEPLOYED VERIFICATION SERVICE / PROCESSOR] receives the purchase token, package,
product, random installation ID and request nonce over HTTPS. It queries Google's
Android Publisher API and acknowledges completed purchases. It returns a signed licence
stored locally, permitting up to seven days offline before an ownership check. Pending,
cancelled or unverified purchases do not create access. Restore purchase uses the
purchasing Google account; a new device/reinstallation requires restore. A refund or
cancellation is checked during renewal/restore, so offline revocation may be delayed.

The application has no hidden gameplay analytics. Purchase tokens, careers and player
names must not enter service access logs, advertising requests or crash messages.

Private career and editor backups are kept on the device to avoid losing progress.
Android cloud backup is disabled. Clearing SCORER app storage or uninstalling removes
its local careers/licence/preferences; files explicitly exported to another document
provider remain until you delete them. Do not uninstall without preserving any data
you want to keep. Restoring a purchase restores access, not a deleted career.
