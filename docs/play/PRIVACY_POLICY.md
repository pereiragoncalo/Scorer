# Scorer World Privacy PolicyPrivacy Policy · Community Rules · Data and Record Removal

Responsible developer: Gonçalo Pereira

Privacy and support: goncalo@clemir.com

Effective: 2026-11-01

Scorer World (com.clemir.scorerworld) is a free football career game with an included local names editor. It has no adverts, advertising SDKs or in-app purchases. This policy covers Scorer World and the retained records of earlier Scorer test builds. The separate Free edition is not a Google Play launch product.

## Automatic usage counts

The current app displays a detailed notice before its first usage transmission. It automatically sends nine numeric counters: app openings and normal closes, new and loaded careers, completed training and matches, goals and save/load failures. Recipient: stats.clemir.com; purpose: understand game usage. Usage is automatic, with a separate legal privacy-request procedure described below. No player name, career, free text, exact event time or device/session identifier is in this payload. These are events, not unique players. Local unsent counts expire after seven days; own server totals are combined by UTC receipt day and kept for 90 days with periodic cleanup. Delays and uncertain acknowledgements can duplicate counts. Older test builds may behave differently: update to the current app.

## Optional public records

Adult-managed access, community rules and explicit Share precede publication of player name, team, completed season, matches, goals and edition/country/division. Use a nickname, never personal contact or school details. New or changed submissions await moderator review; scores are self-reported. A private random ownership token authorizes uploads/removal; the server stores its hash. Opaque public publisher/record IDs support reports and blocking, but cannot authorize deletion. Records remain until withdrawal or moderation.

## Children and local adult controls

Scorer World includes children and teenagers in its audience. Public records are locked by default. An adult must complete a local adult-age declaration and configure/unlock a PIN before sharing; the date of birth is checked locally and discarded, never transmitted. This is a local access safeguard, not verified parental identity or proof of parental consent. An adult should supervise the decision and use pseudonyms, never a child’s full name, school or contact details. Sharing requires community rules and an explicit action; submissions await moderation. Reports, blocking, withdrawal and support are available. Minimal automatic counters do not identify individual children, and the privacy-request suspension also applies to children or requests made by their authorised guardian. We do not collect location, contacts, advertising IDs or behavioural profiles.

Adult PINs are stored as salted hashes on the device. Blocked publisher IDs and safety preferences also remain local. An adult can lock public access and queue withdrawal of records. PINs are never published, exported or sent to the server.

## Reports, moderation and removal

Report/block tools are inside the app. Reports transmit the reporter token, target public publisher ID and a fixed reason; the server keeps the hashed reporter identity, target and reason for up to 30 days with periodic cleanup. Moderators may reject/erase records and suspend publishers. A suspended public ID remains until lifted to prevent re-publication. Stop sharing & remove is available even when public features are locked. Offline removal is retried while the app runs; keep the app until removal completes. If app data or the ownership credential is lost, contact support without sending secret tokens. Uninstalling does not remove public records.

## Included names editor

The editor changes club, league and player display names locally. JSON document imports are previewed before applying. Editing does not change match statistics. There is no automatic upload of name packs or career files. Edited team/player names can appear in a public completed-season record only through the separately enabled record-sharing feature.

## Local careers, exports and backups

Careers remain on-device; there is no automatic cloud career upload or email/password account. World uses phone language locally. Android app backup is disabled. User-selected career exports contain player names and game progress; keep them private and choose a trusted document provider, which may itself use cloud storage. Exports exclude adult controls, usage counters and online ownership credentials. Only the same edition can import a career. Uninstall removes private local files, not exported documents. Managed server recovery backups contain aggregate counters only, exclude public records/reports/privacy requests/tokens, and expire after seven days.

## Transport and Cloudflare

HTTPS validates certificates. The server is operated by the developer and uses Cloudflare Tunnel/CDN. Our collector suppresses IP, user-agent and raw-request logging. This does not mean the network provider processes no personal data.

The developer has confirmed that Cloudflare is used only for Tunnel/proxy routing and protection for stats.clemir.com; Cloudflare Web Analytics, Logpush and other optional collection services are not enabled. Cloudflare necessarily processes IP addresses and connection/security information and may retain service data under its own published terms and privacy policy. Its role varies by data category, including processor for customer content and controller for some service data. Our collector does not retain raw request, IP or user-agent logs. We do not claim Cloudflare retains no logs or that its service-data retention equals our 90-day aggregate retention. No advertising, analytics SDK or third-party marketing tracker is embedded in the app.

## International processing

The developer operates the collector; Cloudflare routes traffic through an international network and may process connection data outside your country, including outside the EEA. EU-only residency is not promised. Cloudflare publishes a Data Processing Addendum and information about European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable to the relevant entities and transfers, Data Privacy Framework participation. These mechanisms apply according to the provider’s actual terms and applicable law; this policy does not claim an individual account audit or a separately verified contract. See the official provider references below and contact the developer about applicable safeguards.

## Legal basis and rights

Where GDPR applies, the developer relies on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) for minimal aggregate usage measurement and service security: understanding which game functions work and maintaining the service. Counters are limited, expire, contain no player/device identifiers and are not used to profile, advertise to or make decisions about a child. Offline play does not require analytics. Continue acknowledges the notice; it is not consent. You may object through Privacy rights, which suspends collection while the request is handled. Applicable rights and any legal requirement for consent take precedence. Optional publication requires a separate adult-managed decision and explicit sharing; legitimate interests support safe operation, moderation and withdrawal of that requested feature. Support and privacy correspondence are processed to answer requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

## Privacy requests and suspension

Open Privacy > Privacy rights to submit a privacy objection or other applicable legal request. The app immediately suspends collection and transmission of usage counters and deletes queued unsent counts, including offline. A request already in transit may complete. Collection stays suspended while the request is pending or granted. A random private case token is created only when you make a request; it is separate from public-record credentials and is never included in usage reports. The server stores its hash, a public reference, status and processing dates, without an email or message. Pending cases remain until resolved; resolved cases are removed after 90 days with periodic cleanup. They are excluded from aggregate recovery backups. Keep the local app data to preserve the suspension. Reinstallation or clearing app data removes the local request, so contact us or submit it again before acknowledging the notice. Only you can withdraw a request using the local credential and explicit confirmation; the server cannot remotely force collection to resume. After a confirmed withdrawal, automatic counting resumes on the next opening. Offline requests retry while the app runs. A damaged local request keeps collection suspended; contact support. For help, email the public case reference, never the secret token.

Contact goncalo@clemir.com for information, access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability where applicable, or assistance with a child’s privacy. We assess requests and normally respond within one month, with any lawful extension explained. We request only proportionate information needed to establish authority and locate the relevant data. Combined anonymous totals cannot be separated into individual contributions. Email correspondence is used to answer the request and retained only as needed for handling and legal obligations. Do not send passwords, PINs, full career files or administrator/ownership tokens. You may complain to your competent data-protection authority, including the Portuguese CNPD (www.cnpd.pt). There is no sale of personal data, behavioural advertising or automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.
