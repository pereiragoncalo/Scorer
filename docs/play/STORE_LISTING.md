## Title

## Short description

Build your striker career around the world. Play, score and edit club names.

## Full description

Create your striker, choose a nationality and starting country, and build a
football career in Scorer World.

Attend your weekly team talk, choose a training focus and prepare for match
opportunities. Take penalties, meet crosses with headers and face the goalkeeper
one on one. Follow domestic leagues and cups, continental club competitions and
official national-team competitions when selected.

Read the match newspaper, earn trophies, receive contract wages and consider
transfer offers. Spend fictional career money on homes, cars, outfits and gifts,
then see your best purchases together in a retro lifestyle portrait. Injuries can
keep you out of matches, and you can choose retirement from age 38.

Your career stays on your device and can be played offline.

The names editor is included at no extra cost. Personalise club, league and player
display names, preview JSON document imports and export your changes. These edits
stay local and do not change match statistics. Export and import your career
separately when moving between installations of Scorer World.

Optional online leaderboards have Season and Career categories. Completed-season
bests are separated by country and division; career records show saved club-career
totals. Sharing a record is separate from viewing the table. Publishing records is adult-managed. Sharing your
chosen name and team requires accepting the community rules and a separate Share
action. New submissions are reviewed before appearing. Report, block and removal
tools are available. Scores are self-reported, not verified competition results.

The game has no advertising and no in-app purchases. Optional usage statistics
are off by default and can be managed in Help > Settings. Online functions require an
internet connection; offline careers do not.

Use Feedback to open a support email draft and send suggestions through your own
email app. No message is sent automatically.

Choose English, Portuguese, Spanish, German, Italian, Korean, Japanese, Simplified
Chinese or Vietnamese in the game. Public privacy, community and removal documents
are provided in English.

Scorer World uses fictional clubs with editable names. No official club affiliation is claimed.
Competitions use the game’s published formats, with simulated qualifiers, league phases and knockout results. It is not a licensed
current football database or a claim to reproduce every real-world regulation,
continental competition or national-team tournament.

## App access instructions for reviewers

No Scorer email/password login is needed. Start a new player, choose a first
contract, attend the weekly team talk, choose a focus, complete the weekly
training and play the available match chances.
The World browser and local names editor are included. The leaderboard is optional,
adult-managed and separate from local game progress. Season and Career submissions appear only after moderation approval.

The administrator token for stats.clemir.com/admin is private operator access;
it is not an app-review credential and must never be included in Console forms.
