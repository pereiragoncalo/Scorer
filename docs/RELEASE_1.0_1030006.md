# Scorer World 1.0 (1030006)

Quiet, nonblocking update checks in the bottom-left splash corner; only a known
mandatory update opens a modal. New players' estate portrait shows no unowned
home or car. Salaries are monthly, with calendar-month payments and compatible
old-contract migration. Player objectives/milestones fit one panel without
scrolling. Match plans favour crosses, free kicks and new long shots, with rarer
one-on-ones and penalties. Shot physics and career totals remain unchanged.

unsigned; existing owner keys stay on bastion. No production-track publication.
