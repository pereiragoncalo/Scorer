## Domestic evidence

- Portugal: reviewed Liga Portugal2026/27 articles21,22,25,26,31; see the source
  catalogue and prior review notes. B teams excluded from promotion to tier1.
- France: complete18-team LiveScore table;1–2 direct,3–5 promotion playoffs,
 16th relegation playoff,17–18 direct relegation. Existing facts document retained.
- EFL: https://www.efl.com/news/2026/march/05/efl-statement--sky-bet-championship-play-off-format/
  Official5March2026 announcement: expansion to six Championship clubs, two
  preliminary games, third/fourth enter two-leg semifinals and a final. It says
  exact final format would be agreed later in2026; this is not a complete current
  handbook review.
- LiveScore compatible public competition pages were retrieved for numerous
  upper/lower divisions. Complete second-tier tables include Portugal, England,
  Spain, Germany, France, Italy, Brazil, Argentina, Scotland, Norway, Sweden,
  Finland, Switzerland, Turkey, Ireland, Czechia, Estonia, Japan and Belgium.
  Table zone labels establish places, not every tie rule or licensing exception.
- Wikipedia revision-pinned season evidence was collected for2026/27Championship,
 2026BrazilSerieB,2026/27SerieB and2026PrimeraNacional. Brazil confirms two separate
  two-leg ties, higher seed at home second and advancing on a tied aggregate.
  Italy confirms conditional rank/extra-time decisions. Argentina confirms two
 18-team zones; that full group season is **not implemented** in the current
  combined-table draft bracket.
